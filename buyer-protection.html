ETHAN MARKET ORDER SUPPORT

Buyer & seller protection

ETHAN MARKET is designed to make marketplace orders traceable and disputes reviewable. Buyers and sellers should keep order details, tracking information and relevant delivery evidence inside their account and support records.

Important: ETHAN MARKET is not an escrow custodian.

ETHAN MARKET does not independently hold customer funds in a bank, wallet or escrow account. Payments and any funds-holding or manual-settlement arrangement must be handled by an authorised payment or banking partner and be approved for the merchant account. An internal payout status is an order-control record; it is not proof that money is segregated or held by a third party.

Payment checkout is only available when the marketplace's payment provider setup is enabled. Availability and refund processing are subject to the provider's terms and applicable law.

How order protection is designed to work

  1. Payment verification. The marketplace records an order as paid only after the payment provider verifies the correct amount, currency and reference.
  2. Seller fulfilment. The seller updates the order as processing or dispatched and supplies a courier or tracking reference where applicable.
  3. Buyer receipt confirmation. After the physical item arrives, the buyer confirms receipt from their order history. Digital orders receive a separate protection window after successful payment verification.
  4. Protection period. The seller payout record remains pending during the configured protection period. The period can be adjusted by marketplace administration.
  5. Dispute review. A buyer can report a problem on a paid order item. This places an unpaid seller payout on hold while the seller and buyer provide information for marketplace review.
  6. Settlement decision. After the protection period and any dispute review, an administrator may approve a seller settlement. The transfer is recorded only after the external bank or payment-provider transfer clears.

How buyers can report a problem

Sign in, open My orders, find the paid item and choose Report a problem. Select the closest reason and describe what happened. Keep photographs, courier receipts, messages and other useful evidence. One case is recorded per order item.

How sellers are protected

Sellers can record processing and dispatch updates, add tracking details and respond to a buyer case. Marketplace administrators review the buyer's description and the seller's response before recording an outcome. Sellers should dispatch only to the order address and retain delivery evidence.

Refunds and payment-provider decisions

Where a refund is approved, the refund must be completed through the payment provider or the relevant payment account. ETHAN MARKET records the resulting reference; it does not promise that a bank or card refund will arrive instantly. Provider processing times, chargebacks and statutory consumer rights continue to apply.
